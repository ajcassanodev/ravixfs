LOCK RULES
  • Each member picks 5 fresh players every week from the open pool. Repeats across members are allowed.
  • Each pick locks at its own player's kickoff. Before kickoff, edits are free and unlimited.
  • After kickoff, a pick can only change by a live substitution — 2 per member per week, same kickoff window, while the game is live. The replacement starts at 0 and the subbed-out player keeps the points already banked.
  • Bye-week or inactive picks score 0. There is no penalty beyond that.
  • Your lock receipt is on the pick row: tap any pick on the dashboard to see when you picked it, when it locked, and whether it came from you or AUTO.
CORRECTIONS POLICY
  • Scores follow the official stats feed. When the official feed revises a total after a game, the site revises the score to match — that is a correction, not a bug.
  • Big-play corrections (sacks, turnovers, touchdowns) appear in the ticker and in the News post for that window. Tackle corrections are quieter: they appear in the corrections feed but never in the ticker.
  • Published News posts are never rewritten. A correction after a post goes out appends a timestamped Correction block to that post.
  • Every point movement is logged with its cause — pick, lock, sub, feed observation, correction, or feed switch — in the audit log. The dashboard's "Why my score changed" section shows your own trail.
  • No commissioner hand-edits: there is no score-editing interface. If a score ever changed with no feed or pick cause, the admin audit view's tripwire would surface it.
