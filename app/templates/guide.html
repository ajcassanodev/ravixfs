How to Play

Welcome to the test season! This is a weekly picks game using NFL defensive players.

Your pick link

The commissioner sent you a private link. That link is your login — don't share it. Opening it signs you in on every Ravix page.

Weekly picks

Each week pick 5 defensive players on the projections page — anyone in the pool, and repeats across members are fine. You can change your picks anytime before each player's game kicks off, right from the projections page or the ✎ icon on your dashboard.

When picks lock

Each player locks when his game kicks off. Pick a Thursday night guy and he locks Thursday; your Sunday picks stay open until their games start. Anything submitted after kickoff doesn't count.

A pick-6 scores 3 + 6 = 9.

Live substitutions (new for Week 7)

Player having a quiet game? You get 2 substitutions per week — the two dashes in your dashboard's MY PICKS header show how many you have left, like timeouts in a football game. Tap SUB on any of your players whose game is live, and pick a replacement.

The rules: the replacement must be playing in the same kickoff window as the player coming out, and his points start at 0 from the moment he enters — his stat line so far is just form. The player you take out keeps every point he already banked, and he's done for the week (he can't come back in). Subs can't be undone, so the dashboard asks you to confirm first. Changing a pick before kickoff is still a free edit on the projections page — subs are only for games in progress.

Bye weeks & injuries

Pick a guy on a bye or who doesn't suit up and that slot scores zero. No other penalty — just check the schedule before you submit.

League News

The League News page is the league's running story. After every set of games — Thursday night, Sunday 1 PM, Sunday 4 PM, Sunday night, Monday night — a recap post goes up with the top performers and what happened to the week's lead (the headline in the ticker at the top of every page links straight to it). The commissioner also posts rule changes and announcements there. Every post is open for replies — jump in.

Test year

We're trying these rules out this season and they might get tweaked. If scoring changes, past weeks recalculate automatically so everything stays fair.
